Imagine an actor standing in front of a green screen, seamlessly blending into a virtual world.
Chroma keying allows filmmakers to replace the green background with anything from a cityscape to a fantastical realm. Chroma key compositing, or chroma keying, is a visual-effects and post-production technique for compositing (layering) two or more images or video streams together based on colour hues (chroma range).
This technique is widely used in weather forecasts and film productions, making editing and visual effects easier than ever.
By using a uniform green background, actors can perform confidently, knowing they'll appear in any setting later.
