Train wheels are designed for efficiency and safety on tracks, not roads.
They have flanges to guide them on the rails, preventing derailments. A train wheel or rail wheel is a type of wheel specially designed for use on railway tracks.
Standardized profiles ensure smooth interaction with tracks, reducing wear. The wheel acts as a rolling component, typically press fitted on to an axle and mounted directly on a railway carriage or locomotive, or indirectly on a bogie (CwthE) or truck (NAmE).
Incorrect profiles increase resistance and risk of accidents.
