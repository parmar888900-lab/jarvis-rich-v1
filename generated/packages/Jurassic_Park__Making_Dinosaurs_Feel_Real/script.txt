In Jurassic Park, groundbreaking CGI and animatronics were used to create the dinosaurs, though the extent of their seamless blend is debated.
Spielberg aimed to portray the dinosaurs as animals, reflecting their natural behavior, a different approach from typical monster movies.
The iconic T-Rex sequence, shot weeks before the end of filming, was so impressive that Spielberg realized the dinosaurs would steal the show.
Jurassic Park used realism and innovation to portray dinosaurs, influencing the industry's standards, according to many critics and industry professionals.
