In The Matrix, the 'bullet time' sequence showed Neo's heightened perception through slow-motion action while the camera moved rapidly around him, redefining action scenes.
The Wachowskis envisioned an action sequence that slowed time while the camera pivoted rapidly around the subjects, and proposed this effect in their screenplay.
This innovative technique allowed the film to explore themes of perception and control, illustrating Neo's enhanced abilities.
The Matrix's bullet time sequence became a template for future action films, influencing everything from Charlie's Angels to Inception.
