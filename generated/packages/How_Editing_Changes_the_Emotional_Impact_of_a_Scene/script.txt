In filmmaking, editing decides the narrative flow, enhancing or altering the emotional response of the audience.
By carefully selecting and arranging shots, editors can amplify suspense, deepen empathy, or shift the mood dramatically. Filmmaking, or film production, is the process of creating a motion picture.
Post-production editing is where the magic happens, transforming raw footage into a cohesive, emotionally impactful story.
