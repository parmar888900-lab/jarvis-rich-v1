Camera movement can dramatically increase tension in a scene, guiding the audience's eye to the most crucial elements.
A slow dolly-in or a sudden pan can heighten suspense, making viewers feel like they're right there with the characters.
Skilful use of camera angles and movement, like a shaky handheld shot, can mimic the protagonist's fear and uncertainty.
By carefully planning and executing these techniques, filmmakers can manipulate the viewer's emotional response, enhancing the overall storytelling.
