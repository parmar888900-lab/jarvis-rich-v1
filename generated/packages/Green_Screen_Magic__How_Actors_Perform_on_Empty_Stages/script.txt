Imagine an actor stepping onto a magical green stage, but what you see is just a backdrop waiting to be transformed.
Chroma key compositing separates the actor from the green screen, allowing filmmakers to replace the background with anything from outer space to a bustling city.
This technique, used in news, movies, and video games, turns a simple green screen into an endless creative canvas.
The result? Scenes come to life with stunning visual effects, making the impossible, possible.
