By moving the camera, filmmakers can create suspense and build tension in scenes, guiding your emotions.
A dolly-in shot brings the camera closer, emphasizing characters or objects, while a whip pan quickly shifts focus, creating a sense of urgency. Filmmaking, or film production, is the process of creating a motion picture.
Camera movements like these are used to manipulate the viewer's perception and keep the audience engaged and on edge.
Watch closely for these techniques in your favorite films to appreciate the artistry behind the scenes.
