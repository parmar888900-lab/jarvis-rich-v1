In 1999, The Matrix introduced 'bullet time', a technique that allowed the action to slow down while the camera moved at normal speed.
This sequence was designed to illustrate characters' ability to manipulate time and space, a core theme of the film.
The Wachowskis proposed the effect in their screenplay, and it became a defining visual element of the movie, influencing future action sequences.
The bullet time technique showcased innovative use of still images and algorithms, creating a revolutionary visual effect that transformed action scenes.
