Actors perform scenes on green screen stages, facing an invisible backdrop. Chroma key compositing, or chroma keying, is a visual-effects and post-production technique for compositing (layering) two or more images or video streams together based on colour hues (chroma range).
Chroma keying technology makes their images transparent, allowing any background to be added later.
Common in weather forecasts and film, this technique lets actors interact naturally with virtual scenery.
Enabling dynamic scenes without physical sets, chroma keying is a powerful tool in modern filmmaking.
