Train wheels are specially designed for smooth track adherence, reducing wear and tear. A train wheel or rail wheel is a type of wheel specially designed for use on railway tracks.
Standardized to 920mm, they fit with rail systems, maintaining safety and performance. The wheel acts as a rolling component, typically press fitted on to an axle and mounted directly on a railway carriage or locomotive, or indirectly on a bogie (CwthE) or truck (NAmE).
These wheels ensure efficient energy use and prevent derailments, critical for train operations.
