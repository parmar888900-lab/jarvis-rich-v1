In filmmaking, green screens transform scenes, letting you blend any background seamlessly.
Chroma keying, or green screen, uses a specific color to make objects disappear, replacing them with other visuals. Chroma key compositing, or chroma keying, is a visual-effects and post-production technique for compositing (layering) two or more images or video streams together based on colour hues (chroma range).
It's widely used in weather forecasts and video games, making it easier to add dynamic backgrounds and environments.
For example, a weather presenter stands in front of a green screen, while viewers see a colorful map behind them.
